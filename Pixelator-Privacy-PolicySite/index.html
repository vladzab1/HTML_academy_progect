Privacy Policy

Pixelator: Retro Art Studio • Effective Date: July 29, 2026

darkV studios ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application, Pixelator: Retro Art Studio (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

a. Photo Processing (On-Device)

Pixelator is a photo editing tool. All image processing, including pixelation, palette application, and framing, is performed locally on your device. We do not upload your photos to our servers, and we do not have access to the images you process within the App.

b. Automated Data Collection (Third-Party SDKs)

To provide and improve our services, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Google AdMob: Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect and use technical data, including:

  • Advertising ID (AAID): Unique identifier for advertising.
  • IP Address: Used to determine approximate location for localized ads.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with ads (clicks, views).
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and performance diagnostics.

For more information on how Google uses your data, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

2. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. Technical data collected by AdMob is shared directly with Google as part of their advertising network operations.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not store your personal information on our servers, we do not have a mechanism for you to request data deletion from us directly. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID and ad personalization settings within your Android device's settings menu.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. We ensure that any data transmitted by third-party SDKs is encrypted using secure protocols (HTTPS). However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not specifically target children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
